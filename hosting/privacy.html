Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

GroceryCompanion is a shopping list, pantry, recipe and meal planning app for households. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who it's shared with, and how you can delete it. GroceryCompanion is published on Google Play and the App Store by its developer (“we”).

In short
  • Without an account, everything stays on your phone.
  • With an account, your household's data is stored with Google Firebase so it syncs between your devices and the people you invite.
  • No ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time, in the app or on the web.

Using the app without an account

Your lists, items, pantry, recipes, meal plans, spending history and preferences are saved only on your device. We don't receive any of it. It's removed when you uninstall the app or use Settings → Delete data on this device.

What we collect when you create an account

DataWhy
Email address and passwordTo sign you in. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us.
Household content: lists and items, pantry items (quantities, locations, expiry dates, barcodes), recipes (including any source links you add), meal plans, purchase prices and stores you enterTo sync it between your devices and with the members of your household.
Household membership: household name, members' email addresses, invite codesTo let you invite people and show who's in your household. Members of a household can see each other's email addresses.
Subscription status (whether Pro is active, which plan, and when it expires)To unlock Pro features for you and your household.

Like most online services, Google Firebase automatically processes technical information such as IP addresses and sign-in times to run and secure the service.

Data that stays on your device

  • Camera. Used only to scan product barcodes. Images are processed on your device and are never stored or uploaded.
  • Region and language. Read from your phone's settings to show prices in your local currency. Not sent to us.
  • Preferences such as theme, household size, dietary choices and monthly budget, and your recently used items. Saved on each device separately.

Who we share data with

We use these service providers to run the app. Each processes data only to provide its service:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Hosting): stores your account and household data. Firebase privacy information.
  • Google Play and the Apple App Store: process Pro subscription payments. We never see your card or bank details.
  • RevenueCat: manages Pro subscriptions. It receives your account's ID and your store purchase records so it can tell the app whether Pro is active. RevenueCat privacy policy.
  • Open Food Facts: when you scan a barcode the app doesn't already know, the barcode number is sent to this open product database to look up the product name. No account or personal information is sent. Open Food Facts privacy policy.
  • Unsplash: the photos for the built-in starter recipes are loaded from Unsplash's image servers, which see your IP address like any website does.

We don't sell or rent your data, share it for advertising, or use it to build profiles. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your sign-in account, your subscription record, and every household you own (with all its content) are deleted immediately. Your membership is removed from households owned by others; their owners keep their shared lists. Google may keep copies in its backup systems for a limited time before they are overwritten.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Pro subscription. Cancel it in your Google Play or App Store account.

Your choices and rights

  • View and edit: everything we store is visible and editable in the app.
  • Delete: in the app under Settings → Delete account, or at grocery-companion-hub.web.app/delete-account.
  • Camera: you can turn camera access off in your phone's settings at any time. Everything except barcode scanning keeps working.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Contact us to make a request; we'll respond within 30 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by Google Cloud. Access rules ensure that only members of a household can read or change that household's data.

Children

GroceryCompanion isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll publish the new version here and update the date at the top.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email the developer contact address shown on GroceryCompanion's Google Play or App Store listing.
