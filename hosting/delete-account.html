Delete your account

You can delete your Grocery Companion account here, or in the app under Settings → Delete account.

What gets deleted
  • Households you own, with all their lists, items, pantry, recipes, meal plans and spending history. Other members lose access to them.
  • Your membership in households owned by someone else. Their owners keep the shared lists.
  • Your preferences, your subscription record and your sign-in account.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. Nothing is kept afterwards. Data stored only on your phone is removed when you delete the app or use “Delete data on this device”. Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a Pro subscription: cancel it in your Google Play or App Store account.
